PSX Racing, CITY edition - map and terrain data: credits and licences
=====================================================================

The CITY edition's map and terrain data are adapted from the sources below:
simplified, re-projected and baked into the game's own formats.

Road network data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  Licence: ODbL 1.0
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Terrain and creek beds: U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program (3DEP)
  Licence: public domain
  https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program
  In: charlotte_dem.bytes, charlotte_city.bytes

Creeks, lakes and ponds in Mecklenburg: Mecklenburg County GIS, CC0 1.0
  Licence: CC0 1.0
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  In: charlotte_city.bytes

Creeks and lakes outside Mecklenburg: U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Hydrography Program (3DHP)
  Licence: public domain
  https://www.usgs.gov/3d-hydrography-program
  In: charlotte_city.bytes

Tree canopy: USDA Forest Service, NLCD Tree Canopy Cover (2024)
  Licence: public domain
  https://data.fs.usda.gov/geodata/rastergateway/treecanopycover/
  In: charlotte_canopy.bytes

Billboard density: NCDOT GIS Unit (statistics only)
  Licence: public record, no licence stated
  https://connect.ncdot.gov/resources/gis/pages/gis-data-layers.aspx
  In: charlotte_signs.bytes
