PSX Racing - map and terrain data: credits and licences
=======================================================

The game's map and terrain data are adapted from the sources below:
simplified, re-projected and baked into the game's own formats.

Road network data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  Licence: ODbL 1.0
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
  In (CITY edition): charlotte_city.bytes, charlotte_bld.bytes, charlotte_routes.json
  In (MAIN edition): the stage roads (Resources/*_stage.json, bogue_*.json)

Terrain and creek beds: U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program (3DEP)
  Licence: public domain
  https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program
  In (CITY edition): charlotte_dem.bytes, charlotte_city.bytes

Creeks, lakes and ponds in Mecklenburg: Mecklenburg County GIS, CC0 1.0
  Licence: CC0 1.0
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  In (CITY edition): charlotte_city.bytes

Creeks and lakes outside Mecklenburg: U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Hydrography Program (3DHP)
  Licence: public domain
  https://www.usgs.gov/3d-hydrography-program
  In (CITY edition): charlotte_city.bytes

Tree canopy: USDA Forest Service, NLCD Tree Canopy Cover (2024)
  Licence: public domain
  https://data.fs.usda.gov/geodata/rastergateway/treecanopycover/
  In (CITY edition): charlotte_canopy.bytes

Billboard density: NCDOT GIS Unit (statistics only)
  Licence: public record, no licence stated
  https://connect.ncdot.gov/resources/gis/pages/gis-data-layers.aspx
  In (CITY edition): charlotte_signs.bytes

Mountain stage terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen/Tilezen); 3DEP and SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey
  Licence: public domain; attribution requested
  https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
  In (MAIN edition): the stages' ground, mountain roads and Bogue Banks (tools/roads, tools/brp, tools/bogue)

Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available here
under the Open Database License (ODbL): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The full registry of sources (including those used only to check the
data, never shipped) is tools/city/SOURCES.md in the game's source
